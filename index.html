One construct per line, the way you name it in the paper. Each is checked against the ledger under the qualification rule from Section 4.3: two names are the same construct only when one name’s token set contains the other’s. Names that merely share a head noun are siblings, not synonyms, and that is where the renaming problem lives.

What the matching does

Names are lowercased, stripped of punctuation and whitespace-collapsed before comparison. A paper-text scan looks for exact occurrences of any canonical name or recorded surface-form variant, up to eight words long. A construct-list check applies three tests in order: exact match on a name or variant; token-set containment either way, which the paper treats as the same construct under a qualifier; and a shared head noun without containment, which the paper treats as a sibling and flags.

What a miss does not mean

The ledger reads abstracts. A construct a paper reasons with but does not name in its abstract is invisible here, so absence from the ledger is weak evidence that a construct is new. Treat a miss as an invitation to make the novelty argument, not as proof of it.

Dissent

Origin and artifact proximity are assigned by majority vote across a construct’s mentions, and the disagreement rate is kept. External validation on fifty documented constructs found that unanimous labels are correct 95% of the time and labels carrying dissent at or above 0.20 are correct in one case out of six. Rows above that threshold are flagged; read them as unresolved rather than as findings.

Propositions

Where two constructs you entered both resolve to the ledger, the page reports how many distinct papers in the corpus assert a relationship between them. Most pairs are asserted by one paper only, so a pair with no assertion is common and is not by itself an opportunity.

What this page will not do

It will not tell you where a new construct should go. The geometric procedure for finding unoccupied regions of the construct space does not recover compositions the field has already made, and that negative result is reported in Section 5.6 of the paper. Until a relational account of composition replaces the midpoint one, a gap suggestion here would be a confident interface over noise.
