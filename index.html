How the ledger is built

A construct is the unit here, and every one of them is recorded with the sentence that asserts it, the role it plays in that paper's argument, and the definition that paper gives it. That record is what makes the rest of this possible.

Deciding when two names mean the same construct

Constructs arrive as names, and the same construct arrives under many names. Merging them needs a threshold, and that threshold is measured on the corpus itself. Pairs of wordings that the extraction gave the same name serve as positives, pairs drawn from different names serve as negatives, and the threshold is the lowest value that holds false merges under one per cent. Management settles at 0.83 and an information systems corpus built the same way settles at 0.84, so the shipped ledger uses 0.84 for both.
